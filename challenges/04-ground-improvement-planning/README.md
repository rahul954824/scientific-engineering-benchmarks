# Ground Improvement Planning

**Domain:** Geotechnical engineering  
**Type:** Conceptual challenge brief

## Scenario

A construction project must improve soft ground before a planned loading date. Settlement plates and pore-pressure instruments provide observations from a trial area.

## Agent task

Estimate relevant soil-response parameters and compare ground-improvement plans that target a specified settlement or strength condition by a deadline.

## Key reasoning challenges

- Separating primary consolidation from other settlement contributions.
- Accounting for spatial variability and uncertain drainage conditions.
- Reconciling measurements from instruments with different error characteristics.
- Comparing construction time, surcharge, spacing and performance constraints.

## Expected deliverables

A parameterized settlement model, predicted time histories, a feasible construction recommendation and a sensitivity assessment.

## Evaluation focus

Correct interpretation of geotechnical quantities, consistency between settlement and pore-pressure predictions, and realistic treatment of uncertainty.
