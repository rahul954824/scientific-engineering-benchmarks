# Membrane Separation Optimization

**Domain:** Separation processes  
**Type:** Conceptual challenge brief

## Scenario

A pilot membrane unit separates a multicomponent gas mixture. The design team must estimate transport behavior and compare feed conditions against product-purity and recovery targets.

## Agent task

Formulate a suitable membrane transport model, calibrate it to pilot observations and identify feasible operating conditions that balance separation performance and equipment area.

## Key reasoning challenges

- Coupled component transport and changing local driving forces.
- Composition- and pressure-dependent membrane behavior.
- Counter-current flow and boundary-condition consistency.
- Distinguishing a true feasible operating window from numerical artifacts.

## Expected deliverables

A model rationale, calibrated transport parameters, predicted product compositions and a constrained operating comparison.

## Evaluation focus

Component balances, boundary-value solution quality, physical plausibility and robustness of the optimization.
