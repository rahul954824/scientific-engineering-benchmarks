# Catalyst Health and Operating Policy

**Domain:** Reaction engineering  
**Type:** Conceptual challenge brief

## Scenario

A catalytic reactor shows gradual performance changes over a production campaign. Temperature and conversion records are available, but some sensors may be biased and the catalyst state is not directly observable.

## Agent task

Estimate a practical catalyst-activity model and recommend an operating policy that balances production targets with thermal and equipment constraints.

## Key reasoning challenges

- Separating catalyst aging from changes in feed or operating conditions.
- Representing heat generation and heat removal consistently.
- Evaluating sensitivity near undesirable thermal operating regions.
- Making decisions when catalyst state estimates are uncertain.

## Expected deliverables

An activity model, diagnostic plots or residual summaries, predicted operating trajectories and a constrained operating recommendation.

## Evaluation focus

Identifiability, energy-balance consistency, safe constraint handling and honest treatment of extrapolation.
