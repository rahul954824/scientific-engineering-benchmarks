# Gas Absorption Capacity Planning

**Domain:** Chemical engineering / separation processes  
**Type:** Conceptual challenge brief

## Scenario

A plant is considering changes to a packed absorption column to meet a higher contaminant-removal target. Pilot measurements cover a limited range of gas and liquid flow rates.

## Agent task

Build a defensible mass-transfer model from the available observations and compare candidate combinations of solvent circulation and packing capacity under operating constraints.

## Key reasoning challenges

- Coupling gas- and liquid-phase resistance.
- Accounting for temperature changes and nonuniform driving force.
- Distinguishing parameter uncertainty from scale-up uncertainty.
- Avoiding designs that meet the target only at a nominal point.

## Expected deliverables

Model assumptions, calibrated parameters, predicted removal performance, a comparison of feasible design options and uncertainty-aware operating margins.

## Evaluation focus

Mass-balance closure, physically meaningful parameters, numerical stability and quality of the design trade-off.
